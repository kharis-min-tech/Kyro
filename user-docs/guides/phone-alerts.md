# Getting alerts on your phone

Kyro can send alerts straight to your phone's lock screen, like a text message, even when Kyro is closed and the phone is in your pocket.

## iPhone

1. **Add Kyro to your home screen.** Open Kyro in **Safari** → **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**. This is required on iPhone and needs **iOS 16.4 or newer**. → [Install on your phone](/getting-started/install)
2. Open Kyro from the **home-screen icon**, not from Safari.
3. Go to **Notifications** and tap **Turn on**, then **Allow**.
4. In iPhone **Settings → Notifications → Kyro**, check that **Allow Notifications**, **Lock Screen** and **Banners** are on.

## Android

1. Open Kyro in **Chrome**.
2. Go to **Notifications** and tap **Turn on**, then **Allow**.
3. Optional: **<Icon name="more" label="more" /> → Install app** to open Kyro like an app.

## Computer

Go to **Notifications**, tap **Turn on** and **Allow**. Alerts appear in the corner of your screen while your browser is open, even if it's minimised.

## Check it really works

<Shot name="notifications-tests" size="wide-card" alt="The Test with your phone locked card and the list of alerts that reach you while Kyro is closed" />

1. On **Notifications**, tap **Send in 20s**.
2. **Straight away**, lock your phone or switch to another app.
3. About 20 seconds later you should see <Icon name="lock" /> **Kyro reached your lock screen**.

If it arrives, you're set. You won't miss alerts.

## What you'll be alerted about

| Alert | Example |
|---|---|
| A room is counted | <Icon name="clipboard" /> Overflow Room counted: 64 people · counted by Grace |
| A room is filling up | <Icon name="warning" color="#f59e0b" /> Main Floor is filling up: 83% full |
| A room is over capacity | <Icon name="alert" color="#ef4444" /> Main Floor is over capacity: 93% full |
| The final count is approved | <Icon name="check-circle" color="#22c55e" /> Final count approved: 1,204 total for today's service |
| A seat is free *(with cameras)* | <Icon name="seat" /> Seat A12 is free: Main Floor |
| The AI has a question *(with cameras)* | <Icon name="question" /> Kyro has a question |

Tapping an alert opens the right page in Kyro.

::: info When your phone is off
Alerts wait for up to 24 hours and arrive when you switch your phone back on. Apple delivers only the **most recent** alert that arrived while an iPhone was off, so check Kyro when you're back for the full picture.
:::

::: warning Do Not Disturb and Focus
If a Focus mode or Do Not Disturb is on, alerts arrive silently. Add Kyro to the apps allowed to notify you during that Focus.
:::

## Turn them off

Tap **Turn off** on the Notifications page. This stops alerts on that device only.
