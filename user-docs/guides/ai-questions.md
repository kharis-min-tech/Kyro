# AI questions & seat alerts

## AI questions

Sometimes the AI isn't sure what it's seeing, for example when someone walks to the front, a seat has been empty for a while, or a group moves forward together. Instead of guessing, it asks you.

<Shot name="ai-question" size="card" alt="A Kyro needs input card asking whether a person moving toward the front is at the stage or altar, with answer buttons" />

### Answering

1. A **Kyro needs input** card appears at the bottom right of any page, with the question, the seat and a picture.
2. Tap the answer that matches what you see, for example:
   - **Yes, it's the stage/altar**
   - **No — toilet break**
   - **Yes — gave their life to Christ**
   - **Yes — count looks right**
3. That's it. **Your answer trains the AI**, so it won't ask the same thing again.

You have **90 seconds** to answer. If more are waiting, the card says **+1 more question queued**.

### Missed one?

If time runs out or you tap **×** (*Skip for now*), the question goes into the **unanswered questions** panel at the bottom right. Tap its header to open it, tap a question, and answer. You can drag the panel out of the way. Unanswered questions disappear after 24 hours.

The sidebar shows **AI has N questions →** while any are waiting.

### Who gets which questions

- **Admins:** questions about the room (*"Is this the stage?"*, *"Does this count look right?"*)
- **Operators and admins:** questions about people (*"Did they leave or take a break?"*, *"Was that an altar call?"*)
- **Viewers:** no questions

## Seat alerts

When the cameras see a seat become free, a **Seat available** card appears at the bottom left with a photo of the seat. Use it to show an arriving guest to a free seat.

- Tap the card to see the photo larger.
- Tap **×** once the seat is filled.

If you've [turned on notifications](/guides/phone-alerts), free seats are also sent to your phone, at most once per seat every 5 minutes.

::: info Needs the camera system
Seat alerts and real AI questions come from the cameras. In Demo mode, AI questions are simulated so you can practise.
:::
