# What is Kyro?

Kyro counts the people at your services and shows where they're sitting, live, so your team can make decisions during the service rather than after it.

Cameras in each room count people as they come in and leave, and watch which seats are taken. Your team fills in the gaps: ushers count rooms that don't have a camera, and staff answer the AI when it isn't sure what it's seeing.

## Two ways to use Kyro

When you open Kyro you choose a **mode**:

- **Demo mode** fills Kyro with realistic sample data: sample cameras, a busy Sunday, AI questions and alerts. Nothing you do affects real data. Use it to learn Kyro and to train new team members.
- **Live mode** is the real thing. It starts empty and shows your actual counts, cameras and history.

See [Signing in](/getting-started/signing-in) for how to choose.

::: tip New to Kyro?
Start in **Demo mode** and follow the [Sunday service checklist](/guides/sunday-checklist). Everything you see in Demo works the same way in Live.
:::

## What Kyro needs to count automatically

Camera counts, seat maps and camera alerts need the **camera system**: cameras connected to the Kyro computer at church, with the Kyro software running on it. Without it, Live mode still lets you record [manual counts](/pages/manual-count), approve totals, send them on through [Integrations](/pages/integrations) and get count alerts on your phone.
