# Finding your way around

## The sidebar

Every page has the sidebar down the left. It lists the pages you have access to, in this order:

**AI Count · Manual Count · Live Cameras · Seat Map · Cameras · Rota · Sessions · Analytics · Seat Editor · Integrations · Notifications · Users**

You only see the pages your role allows. See [Roles & access](/getting-started/roles).

Look out for these signs in the sidebar:

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| An amber banner **AI has 2 questions →** at the top | The AI needs your help. Tap it to go to the Seat Map and answer. See [AI questions](/guides/ai-questions). |
| An amber number next to **AI Count** | How many AI questions are waiting |
| A pulsing green dot next to **Sessions** | A service is being recorded right now |
| The page name highlighted in indigo | The page you're on |

At the bottom of the sidebar you'll find your name and role, the light/dark switch, and **Sign out**.

## On your phone

On a phone the sidebar is tucked away. Tap the **menu button** (☰) at the top right to open it. It closes by itself when you pick a page, or tap the dimmed area to close it.

## Light and dark

Tap **Light mode** or **Dark mode** at the bottom of the sidebar to switch. Kyro remembers your choice on that device. On your first visit it follows your phone or computer's own setting. The sign-in screen has the same switch as a round button in the top right.
