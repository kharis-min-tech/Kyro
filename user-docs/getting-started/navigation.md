# Finding your way around

## The sidebar

Every page has the sidebar down the left. It lists the pages you have access to, in this order:

**AI Count · Manual Count · Live Cameras · Seat Map · Cameras · Camera Mode · Rota · Sessions · Analytics · Seat Editor · Integrations · Notifications · Users**

You only see the pages your role allows. See [Roles & access](/getting-started/roles).

Look out for these signs in the sidebar:

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| An amber banner **AI has 2 questions →** at the top | The AI needs your help. Tap it to go to the Seat Map and answer. See [AI questions](/guides/ai-questions). |
| An amber number next to **AI Count** | How many AI questions are waiting |
| A pulsing green dot next to **Sessions** | A service is being recorded right now |
| The page name highlighted in indigo | The page you're on |

At the bottom of the sidebar you'll find your name and role, **Help guide**, the light/dark switch, and **Sign out**. **Help guide** opens this guide at the page that explains the page you're on.

## On your phone

On a phone the sidebar is tucked away. Tap the **menu button** (<Icon name="menu" label="menu" />) at the top right to open it. It closes by itself when you pick a page, or tap the dimmed area to close it.

## Light and dark

At the bottom of the sidebar, choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Auto**. The sign-in screen has the same three in the top right, so you can set it before you sign in.

**Auto follows your device** — and that's what makes it switch in the evening. A Mac or phone set to change itself switches at **sunset**: about ten past four in November, about twenty past nine in June. Kyro follows it the moment it changes, so a page you left open since the morning goes dark on its own without being reloaded.

Kyro remembers **the choice, not the result** — so Auto stays Auto. It survives reloading, signing out and signing back in, on that device.

::: tip Auto not changing?
Then your device isn't set to change either. On a Mac: **System Settings → Appearance → Auto**. On iPhone or Android: **Display → Automatic**. Kyro can only follow what the device tells it.
:::
