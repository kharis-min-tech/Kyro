# Signing in

Open Kyro and you'll see two cards: **Demo mode** and **Live mode**. Pick one, then sign in.

<Shot name="login" alt="Kyro sign-in screen with the Demo mode and Live mode cards" />

## Demo mode

Demo mode needs no password. It's for exploring and training.

1. Tap **Demo mode**.
2. Tap the account you want to try.

<Shot name="login-demo" alt="Demo mode account list: admin, sarah.usher and james.viewer" />

| Account | Role | Good for trying |
|---|---|---|
| **admin** | Administrator | Everything, including Users, Integrations and the Seat Editor |
| **sarah.usher** | Operator | Running a service: counts, cameras, seat map, rota, sessions, analytics, notifications |
| **james.viewer** | Viewer | The simplest view: Seat Map, Cameras and Manual Count |

Any users you add on the [Users](/pages/users) page in Demo mode also appear in this list.

::: info Demo data stays on your device
Demo data is kept in your browser only. Clearing your browser's storage resets it.
:::

## Live mode

1. Tap **Live mode**.
2. Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
3. Tap **Sign in**.

<Shot name="login-live" alt="Live mode sign-in form with the password shown using the eye button" />

::: tip Check your password before signing in
Tap the **eye** at the right of the password box to show what you've typed, and tap it again to hide it. It doesn't submit the form.
:::

If you see **Wrong password for this account**, check the password with the eye button and try again. If the username isn't recognised, ask your Kyro administrator to check your account on the [Users](/pages/users) page.

## Where you land

- **Administrators and operators** start on [AI Count](/pages/ai-count).
- **Viewers** start on [Seat Map](/pages/seat-map).

## Signing out and switching mode

Tap **Sign out** at the bottom of the sidebar. This also forgets your mode, so the next time you open Kyro you can pick Demo or Live again.

::: warning Signing in lasts for one tab
For safety, a sign-in only lasts while that browser tab is open. If you open Kyro in a new tab or reopen your browser, you'll be asked to choose a mode and sign in again.
:::
