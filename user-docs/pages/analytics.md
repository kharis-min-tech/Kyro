# Analytics

Analytics shows attendance over time: totals, peaks, averages, your busiest days and when people tend to arrive.

<Shot name="analytics" alt="Analytics page with total services, all-time peak, average attendance and average occupancy, an attendance trend chart, arrival pattern and recent services" />

## Choose a camera

Analytics shows **one camera at a time**. Use the **Camera** menu at the top right to switch rooms.

## The summary

| Box | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Total services** | Services recorded in the period |
| **All-time peak** | The highest single-service attendance |
| **Average attendance** | The average, and whether it's going up or down compared with the period before |
| **Avg occupancy** | How full the room is on average |

## Charts

- **Attendance trend:** attendance for each day, with the high and low.
- **Arrival pattern:** average arrivals by hour, with the **Peak** hour.
- **Average by day:** your average for each day of the week.
- **Recent services:** the latest 10, with date, day, attendance and occupancy.

## Filter

- **Only Sundays:** type *Sunday* in the search box.
- **A date range:** set **From** and **To**. Tap **Clear** to reset.

When filtered, you'll see **Showing X of Y days**.

## Look at one service

Tap any row in **Recent services** to open its details: attendance against your 30-day average, occupancy, and how it compared with the same day last week. Close it with **×**.

::: info Manual counts aren't included here
Analytics uses camera counts only. For today's total including manual counts, use [AI Count](/pages/ai-count).
:::
