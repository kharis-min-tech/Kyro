# Seat Map

The Seat Map shows every seat in a room as a dot, coloured by whether it's taken. Use it to find free seats, reserve seats for guests, and answer the AI's questions.

<Shot name="seat-map" alt="Seat Map for Main Floor showing capacity utilisation 79.9%, 72 of 91 seats occupied, and a dot map of seats facing the stage" />

## Choose a room

The tabs along the top are your cameras, named by room. Tap one to see its seats. An outdoor queue camera shows only **People outside now**.

## Reading the map

The stage is at the top, and each row is lettered from the front (A, B, C…).

| Dot | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🔴 Red | Occupied |
| ⚫ Dark | Free |
| 🟡 Yellow | Away briefly: the person has stepped out and is expected back |
| 🟣 Purple | Reserved, or held while the person is on stage, in the choir or at the altar |
| Faded, dashed | In an *ignore* area, so it isn't counted |

The cards along the top show **Capacity utilisation**, **Occupied units**, **Reserved seats**, **Zones known** (the stage, altar or choir areas drawn in the [Seat Editor](/pages/seat-editor)) and **AI questions** waiting for an answer. Use **−** and **+** to zoom the map.

Under the room name you'll see where the map's data comes from: **LIVE** (cameras connected), **NOT CONNECTED**, or **DEMO DATA**.

## Reserve a seat

1. Tap the seat's dot.
2. Tap **Reserve Unit**.
3. Optionally type who it's for, for example *Guest speaker*.
4. Tap **Confirm**.

To change or cancel it, tap the purple dot, then **Edit** or **Remove Reservation**.

## Correct the AI

If a seat is shown wrongly, tap it and choose **Mark Occupied** or **Mark Free**.

::: info This only changes your screen
Marking a seat occupied or free updates the map you're looking at. It's for the moment, and it resets when the page reloads. Reservations are saved.
:::

## Export the seat list

Tap **Download Manifest** under **Unit breakdown** to download a spreadsheet of every seat and its state.

## Reset seats

If the map has got into a muddle, tap **Reset seats** (top right). It clears any seats you marked **Occupied** or **Free** by hand and goes back to what the cameras see.

**Reservations are kept** unless you tick **Also remove reservations**. In Live mode Kyro asks for your password first.

## AI questions on the Seat Map

When the AI isn't sure about something, such as someone walking to the front, a question card pops up. Unanswered questions collect in a panel at the bottom right. See [AI questions & seat alerts](/guides/ai-questions).
