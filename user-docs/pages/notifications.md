# Notifications

Notifications sends Kyro alerts to your phone or computer, even when Kyro is closed and your phone is locked. Turn it on once on each device you want alerts on.

<Shot name="notifications" alt="Notifications page with notifications enabled, alert rules for overcrowding, filling up, AI review questions and camera offline, and coverage by camera" />

For step-by-step setup on iPhone and Android, see [Getting alerts on your phone](/guides/phone-alerts).

## Turn on

<Shot name="notifications-off" alt="Notifications page before turning on, showing Notifications off and the Turn on button" />

1. Tap **Turn on**.
2. When your browser asks, tap **Allow**.
3. A welcome alert arrives: **✅ Kyro notifications active**.

The card turns green and says **Notifications enabled**. Tap **Turn off** at any time to stop alerts on this device.

## Alert rules

| Alert | When |
|---|---|
| **Overcrowding — critical** | A room reaches the critical level (90% by default) |
| **Filling up — warning** | A room reaches the warning level (80% by default) |
| **AI review question** | The AI needs your answer |
| **Camera went offline** | A camera stops sending pictures. Use the switch to turn these alerts off. |

Open **Adjust thresholds** to move the warning and critical levels. They save by themselves and apply to this device.

## Test it

<Shot name="notifications-tests" size="wide-card" alt="Test with your phone locked card with the Send in 20s button, and the list of alerts that reach you while Kyro is closed" />

- **Send test notification → Test:** an alert arrives straight away.
- **Test with your phone locked → Send in 20s:** tap it, then **lock your phone or close Kyro**. About 20 seconds later the alert should appear on your lock screen. This proves you won't miss alerts.

## Which alerts reach you while Kyro is closed

The page lists exactly what can reach you on this setup:

- ✅ Manual counts saved by ushers in Live mode, including **filling up** and **over capacity** warnings
- ✅ **Final count approved** at the end of the service
- ✅ If your phone is off, alerts wait up to **24 hours** and arrive when it's back on
- ⏳ Camera alerts (free seats, room capacity, camera offline, AI questions) start once the camera system is connected

## Demo mode extras

In Demo mode you also get:

- **Automatic demo alerts:** sample alerts (free seats, rooms filling up, AI questions) arrive by themselves **Every 15 min**, **Hourly**, or **Off**. Nothing is sent between 10 PM and 7 AM. Each one is marked *Demo*.
- **Fire demo alerts → Fire alerts:** sends 7 sample alerts about 2 seconds apart. Tap it, then lock your phone to see them arrive.

Live mode never gets demo alerts.

::: warning If it says "Blocked"
You tapped **Block** when the browser asked. Tap the lock icon in the address bar → **Notifications** → **Allow**, then refresh the page.
:::
