# Seat Editor

The Seat Editor is where an administrator can draw the seats onto a camera's picture and mark special areas, such as the stage, altar or choir, so the AI knows what it's looking at.

::: tip You don't have to draw anything
In Live mode **Kyro finds the seats by itself**. Wherever people sit for a while becomes a seat, and empty chairs, benches and sofas it recognises are added too. Within a few minutes of people sitting down, the room's seats appear on the [Seat Map](/pages/seat-map) as a layout called **Found by Kyro**, and the room's seat count is set from them. When Kyro isn't sure (*"Is this a seat?"*, *"Where is the stage?"*) it asks with an [AI question](/guides/ai-questions). Draw seats here only if you want to correct or replace what Kyro found: a layout you save and **Activate** is used instead.
:::

::: info Needs the camera system
The Seat Editor works on a real camera picture. In Live mode, choose the camera at the top: its latest picture from the camera computer loads by itself. Draw the seats where people's bodies are when they sit, then **Save**. The first layout you save for a room starts working straight away: within a few seconds the camera computer shows each seat on the [Seat Map](/pages/seat-map), and the room's seat count is updated to match. In Demo mode, use **Upload camera frame** to try it with a photo.
:::

## Before you start

1. Choose the **Camera** at the top right.
2. Get a background picture: tap **Refresh from camera**, or **Upload camera frame** to use a screenshot from the camera.

## Draw seats quickly (Grid)

Best for rows of seats in blocks.

1. Tap **Grid (auto-generate)**.
2. Enter the **Section name**, the number of **Rows** and **Seats per row**.
3. Drag a rectangle over that block of seats on the picture. It's added when you let go.
4. Repeat for each block.
5. Give the layout a name, then tap **Save layout**.

## Draw individual seats (Manual)

Best for odd layouts. Tap **Manual draw** and drag a small box over each seat. Seats are numbered in the order you draw them (A-1, A-2…). To remove one, tap it, then **Delete selected**. Tap **Save layout** when you're done.

## Mark the stage, altar or choir (zones)

1. Tap **Mark zones (stage, etc.)**.
2. Choose what the zone is for in **Zone is for…**: **Stage**, **Altar**, **Choir**, **Entrance / Exit**, **Toilet / break area** or **Custom…**
3. Drag a rectangle over the area. Zones save as soon as you draw them.

| Zone type | What Kyro does |
|---|---|
| **Stage, Altar, Choir** | *Holds seats*: when a regular goes up front, their seat is kept for them (purple on the Seat Map) rather than shown as free |
| **Entrance / Exit, Toilet / break area** | *Ignore*: activity there isn't counted |
| **Custom…** | You choose: hold seats, or ignore |

Remove a zone with the bin icon in **Zones on this camera**.

## Put a layout into use

Saving a layout doesn't switch it on. Under **Saved layouts**, tap **Activate** on the layout you want. You'll see **Activated** and the number of seats loaded.

::: tip
Use a mouse or trackpad for drawing. Dragging with a finger on a tablet isn't supported.
:::
